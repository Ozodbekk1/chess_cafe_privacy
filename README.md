# Privacy Policy for Chess Café

**Last updated:** November 16, 2026

Welcome to **Chess Café** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience while using our mobile application and services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Chess Café mobile application on iOS and Android platforms.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
* **Account Information:** When you register or sign in (via Google Sign-In or demo credentials), we collect your name, email address, profile picture (avatar), and chosen username.
* **Chess Profile & Performance:** We collect and maintain your chess game activity, including your self-reported or tournament-earned ELO rating, match history, win/draw/loss records, win streaks, tournament rankings, and home cafe preferences.
* **Uploaded Media:** If you choose to customize your profile, you may grant permission to upload an avatar or profile banner image from your photo library.

### B. Device & Hardware Permissions (Used Only With Your Consent)
* **Location Data (When in Use):** We request access to your device's geolocation (`NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription` / `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`) strictly to display nearby chess cafes, venues, and live local tournaments on the interactive map. We do not track your location in the background.
* **Camera Access:** We request access to your device's camera (`NSCameraUsageDescription` / `CAMERA`) solely for scanning physical QR codes at partner cafes to verify your on-site attendance ("Check-in") for chess tournaments. We do not record, store, or transmit video or audio feeds.
* **Photo Library:** We access your photo library (`NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription` / `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`) only when you explicitly choose to upload a custom profile avatar or banner.
* **Push Notifications:** With your consent, we send device tokens to deliver push notifications regarding upcoming tournament registrations, match pairings, and important announcements.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
1. **Tournament Pairing & Matchmaking:** To organize fair Swiss-system tournament rounds and bracket pairings based on verified check-ins and ELO ratings.
2. **Leaderboards & Community:** To compute and publicly display citywide, national, and cafe-specific leaderboards, rank statuses (e.g., Apprentice, Expert, Master), and head-to-head match stats.
3. **Map & Cafe Discovery:** To help you find real-world venues, cafes, and clubs that host chess games.
4. **Account & Security:** To authenticate users, prevent fraudulent check-ins, and ensure a safe, respectful community environment.

---

## 3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We **do not sell, rent, or monetize** your personal data to any third parties or advertisers.

We only share data with trusted infrastructure providers necessary to operate our service:
* **Google OAuth / Firebase:** For secure sign-in and user identity authentication.
* **Cloud & Database Providers:** For encrypted, secure storage of app data, profiles, and tournament records.
* **Legal Compliance:** If required by law, subpoena, or to protect the safety of users.

---

## 4. Data Retention and Account Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v))

We respect your right to control your personal data:

* **In-App Account Deletion:** You have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated personal data at any time directly within the app.
  * **How to delete:** Open **Chess Café** -> Go to **Profile** -> Tap **Settings** (gear icon) -> Scroll to the bottom and select **"Hisobni o‘chirish" (Delete Account)** -> Confirm deletion.
* **Effect of Deletion:** Upon confirmation, your authentication tokens, email, social IDs, personal profile information, achievements, and club affiliations are permanently removed from our active database. Historical tournament results are anonymized to maintain the mathematical integrity of historical leaderboards.

---

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard your personal data:
* All network transmissions between your mobile device and our backend servers are strictly encrypted using **HTTPS / TLS 1.3**.
* User authentication is managed via secure, industry-standard JSON Web Tokens (JWT) stored in hardware-backed secure storage (`expo-secure-store` / iOS Keychain / Android KeyStore).

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Chess Café does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us and we will promptly delete such information.
